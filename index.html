Headline interpretation

  • Absolute fixed-year counts rise at schemes 12d and 12f, but comparison-adjusted results are lower because comparison areas grew faster.
  • The pooled active-travel estimate is about −33% relative to the wider LCR benchmark.
  • The preferred equal-context IMD-matched estimate is smaller, about −9.9%.
  • Weather adjustment changes the headline estimates by less than one percentage point.
  • The small treated sample and uncertain controls mean the results remain exploratory.
