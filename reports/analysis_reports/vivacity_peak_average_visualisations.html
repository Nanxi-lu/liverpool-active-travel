Vivacity Peak Average Visualisations

Generated from vivacity_peak_average_before_after_summary.csv and vivacity_peak_average_route_type_summary.csv.

Grouped bar graphs: Open before/after peak grouped bar graphs.

Metric: pre-installation peak average = highest weekly average before installation; post-installation peak average = highest weekly average after installation; time-to-peak = weeks between installation date and the post-peak week.

Reading caution: this is descriptive peak timing, not causal proof of scheme effect.

Direct comparison: Open direct before/after peak comparison by scheme.

1. Before vs after peak averages

This compares the highest pre-installation and post-installation weekly averages for treated locations, separated by active travel, pedestrian, and cyclist outcomes.

2. Time to post-installation peak

This shows how many weeks after installation each treated scheme/outcome reached its highest post-installation weekly average.

3. Road/path split

This shows the post-installation peak average separately for road and path/cycle-facility countlines.

Underlying tables: scheme-role CSV and route-type CSV.
