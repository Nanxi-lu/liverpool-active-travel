Generated from quality-filtered daily treated rows in the modelling archive. This replaces the previous independent peak comparison for the direct before/after view.

Definition: for schemes installed in 2023, the requested comparisons are 2021 vs 2024 and 2022 vs 2025. For the scheme installed in 2024, the requested comparisons are 2022 vs 2026 and 2023 vs 2025. Values are annual count totals divided by annual observed countline-days. The 2026 comparison is partial to the available data cut-off.

Important: this is no longer a peak-week comparison. It is a fixed calendar-year average comparison, so pedestrian + cyclist is directly comparable with active travel within each year.
